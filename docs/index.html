[01]About

A native terminal that understands your agents.

Vibra is a development workspace for macOS, written in Rust with GPUI and drawn with Metal. It brings persistent terminals, projects, files, Git and your AI agents together in one focused window.

It doesn’t change how you work: the terminal is still the surface, and any CLI runs as is, no integrations required. Vibra groups your sessions by project, keeps track of your agents and tells you when they need you.

Everything happening in your projects, in plain sight.

Agents

Know what every agent is doing

Vibra detects the agents running in its terminals and shows whether they’re working, waiting on you or asking for permission. If it happens in a pane you’re not looking at, you get a notification.
